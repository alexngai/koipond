# Koi Pond Simulator: privacy policy

Koi Pond Simulator is a Chrome extension that shows an animated water scene on the new tab page.

**It collects no personal data.** It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and makes no network
requests of its own: everything it shows is bundled in the extension.

**Settings.** Your choices (scene, time of day, and whether the clock and search box show) are saved
with Chrome's synced storage (`chrome.storage.sync`), so they follow you between computers signed in
to the same Chrome profile. They stay within your Chrome account and are never sent to the developer
or anyone else.

**Search.** When you search from the search box, your query goes to your default search engine
through Chrome (`chrome.search`), exactly as if you had typed it into the address bar. The extension
does not see or keep it.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/alexngai/koipond.
